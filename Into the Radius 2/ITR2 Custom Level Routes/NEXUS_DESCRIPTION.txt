REQUIREMENTS

  UE4SS for Into the Radius 2.
  Developed and tested against UE4SS v3.0.1 Beta (Shipping build).

  Nothing else. Lua only - it replaces no game assets, so it needs no
  pak tools, no LogicMods loader and no other dependency.

  Tested on Steam build 24024260 (executable dated 7 July 2026,
  Unreal Engine 5.5.4 Oculus fork).


WHAT IT DOES

  Gives Outskirts a direct route to the Facility, by retargeting one of
  the two routes Outskirts already has to Forest. Outskirts keeps a
  Forest route through the other, so no connection is lost.

  A second route is included but switched OFF by default: a Forest ->
  Outskirts link near the Facility gate. Returning from the Facility you
  land in Forest about 870 m from the nearest way onward to Outskirts;
  this brings it to about 350 m.

  It costs the Forest -> Peninsula link, because Forest has nothing
  spare near that gate. Peninsula remains directly reachable from the
  Facility. Uncomment one line in config.txt to enable it.

  The config is general - any transition can be pointed at any level.


HOW IT TRAVELS

  It does NOT teleport you. It changes the destination the game's own
  transition reads, then gets out of the way. You shake the Pyotr's
  hand and walk the route exactly as normal, and the game performs the
  transition itself: the fog stage, the inventory handoff, and placing
  you on the correct arrival marker at the other end.

  That matters for compatibility. Nothing about the travel is
  reimplemented, so anything the game does during a transition still
  happens.


COMPATIBILITY

  Safe with:

    - ITR2 Fog Tweaks, ITR2 Distortion Zone Visibility and
      ITR2 NVG Field of View (my other mods). No interaction.
    - Any .pak / LogicMod. This mod replaces no files.
    - Save games are never read or written, and transition actors are
      rebuilt on every level load, so uninstalling restores the game
      completely and immediately.


  Conflicts with:

    - Any mod that edits level transitions or their destinations.
    - A game update that renames the gameplay tags. The mod matches on
      tag names, so a rename means it stops finding the transition -
      no crash, it just stops working, and the config can be pointed
      at the new name.


  Untested:

    - Co-op. Transitions carry flags for host-only travel and for
      requiring all players, and I have not tested a retargeted route
      with a second player. Single player is unaffected.


IS THIS CHEATING?

  It adds a connection between two maps that the game did not include.
  It does not skip content, unlock anything, or bypass a gate - the
  route it takes over is one you already had access to, and you still
  walk it normally.

  If you would rather not shortcut the world map, this mod is not for
  you, and that is a fair position.


NOTES

  Set debug = 1 in config.txt and the log reports which transition you
  are standing nearest to and where it leads. That is how you find tags
  for routes of your own. config.txt ships with every level tag,
  arrival marker and gate tag measured in game.
